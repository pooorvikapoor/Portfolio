Assistant director, producer and creative writer based in Lucknow

I am Poorvi Kapoor, a curious Gen-Z storyteller with a restless appetite for learning and an eye for beauty in both the grand and the overlooked. I am a passionate director. I may not carry decades of experience yet, but I bring versatility, adaptability and the conviction to execute with precision.

I call myself a "Jack of all trades", not to dabble, but to turn ideas into reality with craft and care.
